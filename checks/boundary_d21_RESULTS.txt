Limits (also in the scanner's docstring)
- Text-based. It does not compile Zig, and it does not resolve types through names that are not themselves
  forbidden, so a forbidden type reached only through such a name is found only if that name's declaration is on
  the surface.
- The seven known exposures are an owner decision. The debt list is what lets the gate pass today; removing an
  entry without fixing its exposure makes the gate fail, and so does adding a new exposure.
