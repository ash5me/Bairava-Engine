NOT VERIFIED HERE
  - The Zig parser and importer were not compiled or run (the sandbox has no Zig toolchain). The Python
    model mirrors the parser's rules; it is supporting evidence only, not a gate run.
  - The R8 upload, the sampling shader, and the colour modulation are not implemented or rendered.
  - Channel semantics are taken from the AngelCode format as I understand it (chnl bitmask: 1 blue,
    2 green, 4 red, 8 alpha; common fields: 0 glyph, 1 outline, 3 zero, 4 one). The format document
    could not be fetched from the sandbox, so confirm these against it.

FINDINGS FOR THE IMPLEMENTATION
  a. The existing image path (tools/assets/build.c, build_texture_variants) decodes every image with
     stbi_load(..., 4), builds RGBA8, and encodes BC1 and BC3 variants. A page declared as an ordinary
     image row would lose the R8 format and gain unused colour data. The bitmap_font row must copy its
     page through as a coverage asset, not through build_texture_variants.
  b. The parser stores chnl on each glyph but checks only that it is at most 15. It ignores the common
     channel fields. A missing chnl defaults to 15 (all channels), which is wrong for an R8 page. The
     importer should require chnl=4 on every glyph of this page and redChnl=0 in the common line.
  c. The repository's parser fixture declares alphaChnl=1 and greenChnl/blueChnl=0. That does not match a
     red-only glyph page. Do not copy it into the D-09 asset.
  d. The descriptor cannot hold comments: the parser rejects unknown tags. The coverage meaning must be
     documented in the bitmap_font provider and the NOTICE file, not in the .fnt.
  e. Glyph identity is still the BMFont id as codepoint (no glyph_index field). No change from the
     accepted decision.
  f. The asset is not yet listed in assets/asset_manifest.txt. The bitmap_font row kind does not exist yet.
