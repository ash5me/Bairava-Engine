Scenes and transitions

Use SceneHost(RootScene) to run a scene stack with bairava.run. A scene owns its state and resources; the navigator is borrowed only during update.

A scene supplies:

  • init(ctx) !Self to construct it.
  • update(self, ctx, nav) to update and request a transition.
  • draw(self, dc) to submit frame drawing.
  • deinit(self) or the context-aware teardown hook when resource release needs the context.

switchTo, push, and pop are applied after the current scene’s update returns. A newly requested scene is not updated in the middle of the caller’s update. If initialization of a transition fails, the open scenes remain active and the error is logged.

Do not retain ctx, dc, or nav past the callback. A successful scene initialization is paired with teardown when it is replaced, popped, or the host shuts down. The repository’s public-API scene sample is compiled by the normal build and demonstrates the complete lifecycle.
